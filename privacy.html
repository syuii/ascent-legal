Ascent Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Ascent measures your vertical jump. This policy explains what the app stores, why it is needed, and how you can remove it.

Data you provide

If you create an account, Ascent stores your email address, username, profile name, sport and position, optional profile photo, body measurements and lift numbers, settings, goals, training sessions, and jump measurements.

Camera and videos

Videos are recorded or chosen only when you decide to. Jump analysis runs entirely on your device. Video files stay on your device and are never uploaded. Your profiles, settings, sessions, jump measurements and clip details (such as height and timing) may be backed up to your account.

How data is used

Your information is used to provide measurement, history, profile, account and backup features. Anonymous usage counts are used to decide what to improve. Ascent does not sell personal data, does not use advertising or tracking, and does not post anything publicly without an action from you.

Usage data

Ascent counts how its features are used, such as which mode measured a jump, whether frames were adjusted by hand, which highlight look was saved, and which app a highlight was shared to, along with basic details like phone model, iOS version and app version. These counts go to PostHog with an anonymous ID made for your install of the app. They never include your videos, pictures, jump heights, name, username, email address, or account. PostHog may use your IP address to estimate your country. You can turn this off any time with Share usage data in Settings.

Service providers

Account sign-in and cloud backup are provided by Supabase. Data sent to that service is linked to your Ascent account so it can be restored when you sign in. Anonymous usage counts are sent to PostHog and are not linked to your account.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account in Settings permanently removes the account and its backed-up data. Guest data and videos stay only on your device and are removed by leaving guest mode, deleting individual items, or deleting the app.

Your choices

You can use Ascent in guest mode with no account or backup, decline camera access, turn off usage data, export a summary of your data, sign out, or permanently delete your account from Settings.

Beta waitlist

If you join the beta waitlist on the Ascent website, your email address and the link you came from are stored, only to invite you to the beta. Your email is never shared or used for anything else. To be removed from the list, reply to any email from Ascent.

Children

Ascent is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open Ascent, go to Settings and tap Send beta feedback.
